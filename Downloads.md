# Downloads
A small selection of Tiny d10 content is available for download in PDF format here. Check back frequently, as new material is always being added!

If you're new to the system, it's recommended that you begin with Fantasy Core—and once you've familiarized yourself with the flagship game, come back and check out the zines!
## Fantasy
*[Fantasy Core](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10fc.pdf)* – the complete Tiny d10 tabletop role-playing game system, styled in the fantasy tradition and consisting of three books: *Fantasy Core Rules*, *Fantasy Monsters*, and the one-shot adventure, *The Broken Light*!

>**Note:** The print edition of *Fantasy Core* can be found for sale (at cost) [here](https://www.lulu.com/shop/aaron-carlson/tiny-d10-fantasy-core/paperback/product-vwmmgk.html)!
### Zines
*[The Little Book of Primal Magics](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10pm-zine.pdf)* – a 20-page [[zine-print-settings.png|print-at-home]] zine that introduces two new classes, eight new races, additional rules, and more!

*The Worldwide Adventure Generator* – a 22-page ([print-at-home](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10wag-zine.pdf) or [electronic PDF](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10wag.pdf)) zine that presents a collection of genre- and system-agnostic procedures and tables for powering adventures across the [6-Mile Hex Atlas of Earth](https://idraluna-archives.itch.io/6-mile-hex-atlas-of-earth) on the fly!
## Miscellaneous
*[Character Sheet (Fantasy)](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10cs.pdf)* – an elegant character sheet best suited for fantasy-style play [^1].

*[Character Sheet (Generic)](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10cs-generic.pdf)* – a simple character sheet suitable for all styles of play.

*[Character Sheet (Generic – Fillable)](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/c70f8f52c3adece09113e3690dfbf6c7/Files/Documents/td10cs-generic-fillable.pdf)* – a simple, fillable character sheet suitable for all styles of play.

[^1]: created by [Jens](https://x.com/JensD29).
